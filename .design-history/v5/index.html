PHOTOGRAPHY.
DIRECTION.
A POINT OF VIEW.

I’m Peter. I make photographs and direct stories with a feeling all their own.

I came to photography through a love of directing. Light, color, and composition became the way I explored that instinct — creating worlds that feel a little unexpected, and images you want to spend time with.

From a conceptual fashion campaign to an intimate portrait, the approach is always personal. Your idea, a fresh perspective, and care in every frame.

01Photography+

Fashion, beauty, product, and editorial imagery. Thoughtful lighting and a visual language shaped around each project.

02Direction+

Bringing an idea into focus through visual storytelling, performance, and the details that make a scene feel alive.

03Concept to creation+

A collaborative process, from the first conversation and moodboards to shoot planning and the final images.
